# Poyo — Official Website

A Ukrainian fan site dedicated to **Poyo**, the beloved character who is... well, a Ukrainian guy whose only talent is being silly. 🥴

> *"Just Poyo being Poyo."*

---

## 📖 About

This website was created for **Poyo** — a Ukrainian character known for his uniquely chaotic and delightfully dumb personality. There's no deep lore here, no epic backstory. Just a guy, being a goofball, and we love him for it.

The site serves as a small tribute to the character and everything he represents: pure, unfiltered, Ukrainian silliness.

---

## ✨ Features

- Simple, clean presentation of the Poyo character
- Ukrainian-flavored theme
- Lightweight and easy to browse
- Built with love (and a little bit of chaos)
